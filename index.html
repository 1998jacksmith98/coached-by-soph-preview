About me

I’m Soph.

I help women get stronger, gain confidence and build healthier habits without fitness taking over their life. Sustainable results, no extremes. Beginners welcome.

Whether you’re new to the gym, looking to change your physique, training towards a challenge or simply wanting to feel your best, I want to help you find a way of training that you genuinely enjoy and that fits into your life.

1:1 online coaching and in-person sessions in Essex. I also run structured programmes like the 90-Day Winter Lock-In — three gym workouts a week, progressive phases, beginner-friendly.

The aim is consistency over intensity, and results that last.
